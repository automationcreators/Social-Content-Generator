## Body Section

### Point 1: WHY I Changed My Mind About AI Tools

**WHY it matters:**
Two years ago, I was the person recommending every new AI tool to my portfolio companies. "Try this for content." "Use this for customer service." "This one's great for data analysis."

The result? Tool sprawl. $300-500/month in subscriptions per company. And minimal ROI.

Then I noticed something in the companies that were actually getting results: they weren't collecting tools. They were building systems.

**[VISUAL: TOOL SPRAWL → SYSTEM FOCUS]**

**WHAT the data showed:**
Across 23 implementations, companies using 5+ AI tools averaged 2x productivity gains.

Companies using 1-2 tools with documented skills? 20-30x productivity gains.

Same AI underneath. Completely different architecture.

**HOW I discovered this:**
One of my portfolio companies—a $2M SaaS—had a single team member who was outperforming the entire content team. Same tools available to everyone. The difference? She had built what she called "knowledge templates."

Every task she touched, she documented her process, her preferences, her examples. Then she fed that to Claude ONCE. After that, the AI knew her work, her voice, her standards.

She wasn't prompting anymore. She was delegating to a system that knew her business.

**Pattern Break:** "What she had built—accidentally—was exactly what Anthropic just launched as a formal product. And that leads to the second point..."

### Point 3: HOW I Build AI Skills in Portfolio Companies

**WHY it matters:**
The companies I've scaled from 6 to 8 figures all share one trait: they systematize before they scale. AI skills are just the latest evolution of that principle.

After helping implement this across 47 companies, here's the exact sequence that works:

**[VISUAL: SYSTEMATIZE → SCALE | 47 IMPLEMENTATIONS]**

**WHAT the framework looks like:**

**Step 1: The Expertise Audit (Day 1)**
Before building any skill, you identify where expertise currently lives:
- Who's the best at each repeated task?
- What do they know that others don't?
- What patterns have they internalized?

In one portfolio company, we discovered their best sales rep had 47 specific phrases she used in cold outreach. Nobody else knew them. That undocumented expertise was costing them $200K/year in underperformance.

**Step 2: The Documentation Sprint (Days 2-5)**
You extract that expertise into structured documentation:
- Process steps (what happens in what order)
- Decision criteria (how to choose between options)
- Quality standards (what good looks like)
- Examples (specific outputs that represent success)

This is the hard work nobody wants to do. It's also where 90% of the value comes from.

**Step 3: The Skill Build (Days 6-7)**
Package the documentation into a functional skill:
- Reference files for context
- Clear input/output specifications
- Auto-save to appropriate locations
- Integration with existing workflows

**HOW to start this week:**

**Monday:** Pick your highest-leverage repeatable task
**Tuesday:** Shadow your best performer for 2 hours
**Wednesday:** Document their process in simple markdown
**Thursday:** Build a basic skill with that documentation
**Friday:** Test it 5 times and gather feedback

**Closer:** "This framework has generated over $3.2M in additional capacity across 8 portfolio companies—without hiring a single new person. The leverage is real."
