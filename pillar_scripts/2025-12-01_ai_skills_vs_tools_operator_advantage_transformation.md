### Point 3: HOW You Can Build This (This Week)

**WHY it matters:**
I'm not going to pretend this is a 10-minute setup. But I will tell you it's a ONE-TIME investment that pays dividends forever.

Here's the exact process I used, broken into 5 days:

**[VISUAL: 5-DAY BUILD | ONE-TIME INVESTMENT | FOREVER DIVIDENDS]**

**WHAT to do each day:**

**Day 1: The Expertise Capture (2 hours)**
Pick your ONE highest-leverage repeatable task. For most people, this is content creation, client communication, or data analysis.

Ask yourself:
- What do I know that I keep re-explaining to AI?
- What patterns have I internalized that I can't articulate?
- What does a "great" output look like for this task?

Write it all down. Messy is fine. You're capturing, not organizing.

**Day 2: The Structure Pass (1 hour)**
Take your messy notes and organize them into:
- Process (what steps happen in what order)
- Preferences (what choices you always make)
- Examples (3-5 outputs that represent your quality bar)
- Non-negotiables (what must never be wrong)

This becomes your reference documentation.

**Day 3: The Skill Build (2 hours)**
Create your first skill:
- Clear description of what it does
- Reference to your documentation
- Input specification (what you'll provide)
- Output specification (what it should produce)
- Save location (where outputs go automatically)

If you're using Claude, you can do this with a SKILL.md file.

**Day 4: The Testing Sprint (1 hour)**
Use your skill 5 times on real tasks. Document:
- What worked perfectly
- What needed correction
- What was missing

Refine based on feedback.

**Day 5: The Expansion Plan (30 minutes)**
Identify your next 3 skill candidates. Start the cycle again for the next highest-leverage task.

**HOW this compounds:**
- Week 1: 1 skill, 3 hours saved/week
- Month 1: 4 skills, 12 hours saved/week
- Month 3: You've built a system that runs your routine work

**[VISUAL: WEEK 1 → MONTH 3 | COMPOUND GROWTH]**

**Closer:** "Six months from now, you'll either be 15 hours ahead every week, or you'll still be starting from scratch every morning. The work you do THIS week determines which path you're on."
